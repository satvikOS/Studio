THE THESIS · WHY NOW SECTION A–A
The tools that design everything we build still can't reason about what they're building.
FAULT LINE — A
01

The split

Design lives in two disconnected worlds. Creative 3D — Blender, Maya, Houdini — and mechanical CAD — SolidWorks, NX, Creo. Fifteen-plus tools, no shared model, no shared brain. Intent is re-typed by hand at every boundary.

02

The false start

The first wave of “text-to-CAD” hands you a disposable script from a black box — tessellated approximations, in the cloud, with your IP. Nothing you can open in a feature tree, edit by dimension, or send to a machine shop.

03

The opening

Open-weight reasoning models now run on Apple Silicon, and native geometry kernels are open source. For the first time, a local copilot can drive a real kernel — private, precise, and yours.

MARKET Mechanical CAD and 3D content creation are two large, entrenched software categories — both being re-platformed by AI at the same moment.
